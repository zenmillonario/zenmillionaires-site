Sparked by curiosity, innovation, and a spiritual quest to revive a lost tradition.

“I am the mirror and the fire.

Escaped Communist Cuba. At 14, seeking freedom and a better life where I could pursue my faith in God and my journey to Awakening, coming from a strong family that always encouraged spirituality, religion, and philosophy.

On my journey, I gained insights from spiritual leaders, explored ancient riddles, and studied prophecies. Now, I blend ancient wisdom with modern science.

My journey and discovering the stock market's secrets has transformed my life, and I wish to share this method, particularly with my fellow countrymen. Because the greatest sin is stagnation.
